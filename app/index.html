      Platinum roadmap — Vice Got a Price!

      One playthrough is enough (PowerPyx). No difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

      1. Step 1 — Chapter 1, do the one missable thing. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. ⚠ POSSIBLY MISSABLE — Positive Mental Attitude (PowerPyx: unconfirmed whether it is required, doing it is the safe choice). Then finish Chapter 1.
      2. Step 2 — Play the story, Chapters 2–4. Finish every main mission and the named mission trophies (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). Chapters 2+ open the partner trophies and free roam. Scan new vehicles and study animals with binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist counts progress made during the story.
      3. Step 3 — Side strands. Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and complete 8 stranger mission strands (Strangers & Freaks). Hunt Bret's unique animal. These stay available after the credits.
      4. Step 4 — Activities grind. 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range gold medals, 20 race wins, drone races at all 14 locations, fishing vs Cal, one arcade game, bowling, 50 challenges. Try each of the 9 activity types once for Master of None.
      5. Step 5 — Robberies & money. 5 bank vaults, 1 armored truck, 1 illegal operation per gang (9 gangs), 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, a jewelry store undetected, 4-star wanted escape with your partner. Launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all 8 garages.
      6. Step 6 — Collectibles & exploration. 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, explore all 52 areas, meditate at all 18 spots, register 10 vehicles in Scooter Bros.
      7. Step 7 — The 100% checklist, then platinum. Open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion and finish it (A Long Road, gold). It is lenient — about 60% of everything, roughly 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges. The platinum pops with the last trophy.

      Missable trophies — the full list

      Only one is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia. Every other trophy is confirmed obtainable after the story in free roam, and nothing toward 100% is missable (PowerPyx, GTABase). A few optional missions and magazine collectibles disappear, but they do not affect any trophy.
